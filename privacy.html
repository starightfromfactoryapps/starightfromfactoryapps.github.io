Journal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Journal is a personal journaling app for Android, made by Haya-developer ("we"). This policy explains what the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: your journal stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive. We don't run any servers, and we never see your entries.

1. What Journal stores, and where

WhatWhere it's keptWho can see it
Your journal entries, tags, day markers and settings (such as your start date and voice language)On your phone, inside the app's private storageOnly you, on your phone
A backup copy of the aboveA hidden app folder in your own Google DriveOnly you (through the app). We cannot access it.
The email address of the Google account you sign in withOn your phone, so the app knows whose journal to show and where to back it upOnly you, on your phone

Journal has no servers. Nothing you write is sent to us, and we have no copy of it.

2. Google sign-in and Google Drive

To use Journal you sign in with your Google account. Journal asks Google for one permission only:

  • "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive" (drive.appdata).

This lets Journal save and read its own backup file in a hidden folder of your Google Drive. Journal cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and it does not read your Gmail, contacts, photos or anything else in your Google account. Journal uses your account's email address only to show which account is signed in and to keep each account's journal separate.

Backups happen when you tap a backup button, when you sign out, and automatically once a day while you're signed in and online (you can switch the daily backup off in the app).

Journal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from your Google account is used only to provide Journal's backup and restore features to you. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone else, and not read by people.

3. Voice typing

If you use the Speak button, Journal uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (on most phones this is provided by Google) to turn your speech into text. Your voice is processed by that service under its own privacy policy (for Google: Google Privacy Policy). Journal does not record, store or send your audio anywhere itself; it only receives the resulting text, which you can review before it is added to your entry.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone: only for voice typing, and only while you are using the Speak feature. Asked the first time you tap Speak; you can refuse and type instead.
  • Internet: only for Google sign-in and for backing up to and restoring from your Google Drive.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No access by us to your entries, your backups or your Google account.

6. Keeping or deleting your information

  • Sign out (Settings → Google Drive backup → Sign out): hides that account's journal on the phone until you sign in again. It also withdraws Journal's Drive access for that account.
  • Uninstall Journal: deletes everything Journal stored on the phone.
  • Delete your backup from Google Drive: open drive.google.com → Settings (gear) → Settings → Manage apps → Journal → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Journal's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Journal and remove access.

Because we don't hold any of your information, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

Your journal on the phone is protected by Android's app storage, which other apps can't read. Backups travel to Google Drive over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are stored under your Google account's protection.

8. Children

Journal is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
